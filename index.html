INDIAN PREMIER LEAGUE (IPL)

The Indian Premier League is a professional Twenty20 cricket league in India organised by the Board of Control for Cricket in India. Founded in 2007, the league features ten state-based or city-based franchise teams.

The IPL is the most popular and richest cricket league in the world and is held between March and May

  • Founded: 2007, India
  • First edition: 2008
  • Format: Twenty20
  • Headquarters: Cricket Centre, Churchgate, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
  • Most successful: Chennai Super Kings; Mumbai Indians; (5 titles each)
